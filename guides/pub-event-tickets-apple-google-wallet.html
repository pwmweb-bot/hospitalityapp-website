Guide · Event tickets

Tickets, in the wallet.

Updated 30 October 2026.

Quick answer. You can sell quiz night, live music and tasting tickets on your own site. The guest can add the ticket to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet from the confirmation email or the paid page. HospitalityApp takes no ticket fee. Tickets are included on every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. Card payments go to your own Stripe account, and Stripe’s own fees still apply. Staff still scan the ticket’s QR at the door.

Events and tickets Ticket help

Who it is for.

This is for a UK pub, bar or restaurant that sells its own nights: a quiz, a band, a tasting, a supper club. The tickets should live on the venue’s site, not on a ticket agency that takes a cut of a £8 quiz ticket. The same system already runs the till, so a drink that is included in the ticket can be rung at £0 rather than written on a pad.

It is a poor fit if you need a national box office, allocated seating across a large theatre, or a marketplace that sells the night for you. HospitalityApp sells the night for the venue, on the venue’s page.

What you can put on sale.

Tickets can be free or paid. Types already in the product are standard, early bird and table, and a ticket can also be free. Each type has a price, a limit and a time it goes on sale. The sale can be scheduled, with a countdown on the event page. At checkout you can ask what you need, such as dietary needs or team names.

The venue can set a booking fee and the VAT on that fee. Payments, including that fee, go to the venue’s own Stripe account. HospitalityApp takes no commission on tickets. Stripe’s own fees still apply. There is no ticket fee from us.

Each ticket has a unique QR, emailed to the guest. From the confirmation email or the paid page, the guest can add the ticket to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Staff still scan that QR at the door from a phone. The scanner flags a ticket that is already scanned, refunded, or for the wrong event. The pass is a convenience for the guest. The door check is still the QR.

On the night.

Events can run past midnight, which matters for a pub that closes after twelve. You can refund selected tickets. A website embed snippet puts the event on a page you already have. Food or drink included in the ticket is rung on the till at £0, so the kitchen and the stock still see the item and the guest is not charged twice.

Tickets are included on every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. You do not move up a plan to sell a quiz. The till, if you want included drinks rung through it, is on Complete at £149 a month + VAT. New customers start with a 30-day free trial of Complete Plus. No card is needed.

What this is not.

The rota diary can hold a function, a sports fixture or a private party, with covers, a brief and a task list, beside the shifts. That is an internal note for the team. It is not ticket sales. Selling the night is events and tickets. Hiring out the function room by the session, with a deposit, is Spaces. The three can sit on the same Friday. They are not the same screen.

Loyalty is separate again. A regular who buys a quiz ticket can also hold a loyalty card in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, scanned at the till, with points per £ of the bill. Tips do not earn points. Loyalty is on every plan and off until you switch it on. It is not attached to the ticket automatically. See the pub loyalty guide.

Questions people ask.

Is there a ticket fee?

No ticket fee from HospitalityApp, and no commission on the sale. Payments go to the venue’s own Stripe account. Stripe’s own fees still apply. The venue can set its own booking fee, and the VAT on that fee.

Can guests add the ticket to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet?

Yes. From the confirmation email or the paid page, the guest can add the ticket to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Staff still scan the ticket’s QR at the door. The scanner flags tickets that are already scanned, refunded, or for the wrong event.

Which plan includes event tickets?

Every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. A 30-day free trial of Complete Plus is available, with no card needed. Included food or drink is rung on the till at £0, and the till is on Complete at £149 a month + VAT.

Can a ticket be free, or a table?

Yes. Ticket types include standard, early bird and table, and a ticket can be free. Each type has a price, a limit and a time it goes on sale. Checkout can ask for dietary needs or team names.
