Guide · Loyalty

Loyalty, on the bill.

Updated 30 October 2026.

Quick answer. A pub loyalty app that works with the till keeps the card in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Staff scan the QR. Points earn per £ of the bill, at a rate the venue sets, and tips do not earn points. HospitalityApp includes loyalty on every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. It is off until you switch it on for that venue.

Loyalty The till

Who it is for.

This is for a UK pub, bar or restaurant that wants regulars to carry a card, and wants the points to come off the bill the till already took. A stamp card in a drawer, or an app the bar staff have to open separately, gets abandoned on a Saturday. The card needs to be on the phone the guest already has, and the scan needs to happen on the till or on a staff phone.

It is not a discount club you have to join a paid plan to unlock. Loyalty is included on every plan, from Essentials at £49 a month + VAT. It stays off until a manager switches it on for that venue, so a site that does not want it is not running it.

How HospitalityApp does it.

The guest gets a loyalty card in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with a QR for staff to scan. Points earn per £ of the bill, at a rate the venue sets. Tips never earn points. That split matters under the tips rules a pub already has to keep: a tip is not a sale.

Rewards can be a points discount through the existing discount button, or a product stamp. A stamp award is the familiar one: buy five pints of a named beer, get one free, with progress on the card, such as three of five. Redeeming a stamp adds the free item at £0, and the VAT is handled. Staff can scan from the Scan button in the staff app, or from an open bill. They can also scan after payment, to attach the card when loyalty is on and no card is on the bill yet. Managers can adjust balances.

Points can be scoped to one site or to a group of sites. A regular who drinks at two of your pubs can carry one card if you set it that way. Multi-site is already on every plan. There is no per-head fee inside the staff allowance, and no per-terminal fee on the till.

What it does not do.

The till records the sale. Your own card machine takes the money. Loyalty does not change that, and it does not take the card payment. Online deposits and event tickets are the payments that go through Stripe, into the venue’s own account, with no commission from us. A loyalty point is not one of those payments.

A ticket for a quiz night can also sit in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. That pass is the ticket, scanned at the door. It is not the loyalty card. The two can both be on a guest’s phone. They are different QR codes, on different features, both included on every plan. Tickets are described in the event tickets guide.

Price, plainly.

Essentials is £49 a month + VAT per venue, with 10 staff, and includes loyalty, the rota, clock-in and basic checklists. Professional is £99, with 20 staff, and adds the full compliance suite and payroll reports. Complete is £149, with 30 staff, and adds the till, which is where the QR is scanned on the bar. Complete Plus is £199, with 50 staff, and adds mobile ordering, the kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans. Annual prices are £490, £990, £1,490 and £1,990. A 30-day free trial of Complete Plus needs no card.

You do not pay HospitalityApp a fee per point or per reward. The rate per £ is yours to set. If you never switch loyalty on, nothing is earned.

Leave it off if you do not want a scheme. A loyalty card that staff forget to scan is worse than no card, because regulars notice. Switch it on when the bar will actually scan the QR, set a rate you can afford, and decide whether stamps or a points discount match how you already look after regulars. Both are in the product. You do not have to use both.

Questions people ask.

Do tips earn loyalty points?

No. Points earn per £ of the bill, at a rate the venue sets. Tips never earn points.

Where does the card live?

In Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with a QR that staff scan on the till or from the staff app. Loyalty is off until you switch it on for that venue.

Which plan includes loyalty?

Every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. Scanning the card on the till needs the till, which is on Complete at £149 a month + VAT. Staff can also scan from the staff app.

Can one card work across more than one pub?

Yes. The venue can scope points to one site or to a group of sites. Managers can adjust balances. A stamp reward, such as buy five and get one free, shows progress on the card, and the free item is rung at £0.
