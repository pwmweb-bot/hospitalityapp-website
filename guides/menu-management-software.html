What it is

Menu management, defined.

Menu management software is where you build and maintain your product list — categories, items, prices, measures and costs — in one place. The point is that the same menu drives everything downstream: the till buttons your staff tap, the tickets that print in the kitchen, and any QR or online menu a guest scans. Change a price once and it changes everywhere.

It isn't a printed-menu designer, and it isn't a spreadsheet you re-type into the till. When the menu and the till are two separate things, they drift — a happy-hour price that never came off, a new dish the kitchen never saw. One menu, one source of truth, no drift.

The checklist

Seven things menu management must do.

1 · Categories & sub-categories

Draught, bottles, wine, spirits, food — and sub-groups within them — so the till stays fast to navigate at a busy bar.

2 · Multiple measures per item

Pint and half, single and double, 175/250/bottle. One product, several measures, each with its own price.

3 · Cost as well as price

Enter what a pour or plate costs you and the software shows gross-profit % per item — the number that tells you what to reprice.

4 · Per-item VAT

20% on most, zero-rated where it applies. Receipts and reports split VAT without a separate spreadsheet.

5 · Drives the kitchen

Food items route to the right kitchen station and print as prep tickets — from the same menu the till uses.

6 · Availability toggles

86 a dish in one tap. It greys out on the till and the QR menu at once, no crossings-out on a printed card.

7 · One edit, everywhere

Change a price or add a special once. Till, kitchen display and QR menu all update — no re-keying, no version drift.

The number that matters

Gross profit, per product.

Most venues know their overall GP roughly, from the accountant, a quarter late. A menu that carries costs as well as prices flips that around: you see gross-profit percentage on every line, live. The £6 cocktail that costs you £2.40 is doing more for you than the £5.20 pint that costs £3.10 — and until you can see it per product, you're repricing in the dark.

"We don't know our costs precisely."

Start with your best estimate per item — you'll be close enough to spot the outliers, and you can tighten the numbers as your stock counts come in. An approximate GP on every line beats an exact GP on none.

"Do measures each need their own cost?"

Ideally yes — a double isn't twice the GP of a single once you account for the pour. Per-measure cost keeps the percentage honest across the whole range.

"How does this connect to stock?"

When a sale depletes stock automatically, the same item costs feed stock valuation and low-stock alerts — so the menu, the till and the stockroom agree.

FAQ

Menu management, asked & answered.

What is menu management software?

Where you build your product list once — categories, items, prices, measures, costs — so the same menu drives the till, kitchen display and QR menu, and a price change updates everywhere.

Does it handle pints, singles and wine measures?

Yes — one item can carry multiple measures, each with its own price and cost, so the till shows the right buttons and GP is right per pour.

Can it track gross profit per item?

Yes, when each item and measure carries a cost as well as a price. GP% shows on every line so you can reprice what isn't working.

Does it handle VAT per item?

Yes — set the VAT rate per item so receipts and reports split VAT correctly without a separate spreadsheet.
