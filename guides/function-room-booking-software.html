Guide · Function rooms

Book the function room.

Updated 30 October 2026.

Quick answer. Function room booking software lets a guest book a function room, boardroom or snug by the session or by the hour, and pay a deposit when they book. In HospitalityApp that is included on every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. The deposit goes to your own Stripe account. HospitalityApp takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. The Crown & Anchor is the pilot.

Spaces and function rooms Spaces help

Who it is for.

This is for a UK pub, inn or hotel that hires out a room as a whole: a function room for a wake or a wedding breakfast, a boardroom for a morning meeting, a snug for a private party. The booking is the room, not a table for two, and not an overnight bedroom. The guest should be able to see what is free and pay a deposit without a phone tag with the manager.

The Crown & Anchor is the pilot. Screens in the product and the help guides use example data from that pilot. They are not a customer quote.

How a booking is set up.

You set the room up once. Layouts are theatre, boardroom and banquet, and each layout has its own capacity, so the numbers match the way the room is set. Sessions can be morning, afternoon, evening, or by the hour. Set-up and clear-down time is kept free, so one booking does not run into the next. Dates can be blocked.

Charging can be room hire, a minimum spend, a price per person, or a mix. Extras such as coffee, a buffet or a screen can be priced per person or per booking. A space can be enquiry-only instead of instant book, if you would rather approve a Saturday before it is confirmed.

Guests book on a page in the venue’s colours. They can pay a deposit or the full amount by card, into the venue’s own Stripe account. HospitalityApp takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. The confirmation email carries the date, the session, the layout, what was paid and what is left, plus the cancellation terms the venue sets.

Where it sits in the rest of the venue.

Spaces is separate from overnight bedrooms and from table reservations. Bedrooms, the room diary and bar or food on the room bill are room management, included on every plan, with charges to the room needing the till on Complete. Table reservations and floor plans, including an optional card deposit on a table booking, are on Complete Plus at £199 a month + VAT. Do not put a function-room deposit on that plan. The function-room deposit is on every plan, from £49 a month + VAT.

The team still needs to know the room is booked. Functions and events can sit alongside the rota, so the people on shift see what is coming. That diary note is not the guest booking and it is not a ticket. Ticketed nights, with a QR and a wallet pass, are events and tickets, also on every plan, with no ticket fee from us.

Price.

Spaces and function rooms are included on every plan. Essentials is £49 a month + VAT per venue, with 10 staff. Professional is £99, with the full compliance suite and payroll reports, and 20 staff. Complete is £149 and adds the till, with 30 staff. Complete Plus is £199 and adds mobile ordering, the kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans, with 50 staff. Annual billing is £490, £990, £1,490 or £1,990, paid up front, which saves two months. A 30-day free trial of Complete Plus needs no card.

There is no per-booking fee from HospitalityApp and no commission on the deposit. The money lands in the venue’s Stripe account. Stripe’s fees are Stripe’s.

A manager still sees every booking next to the rest of the venue, rather than in a notebook behind the bar. Change a booking, or cancel it, from the same place the guest used. Questions people ask.

Can guests pay a deposit when they book?

Yes. They can pay a deposit or the full amount by card, into the venue’s own Stripe account. HospitalityApp takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. A space can also be enquiry-only, with no payment until you confirm it.

Which plan includes function rooms?

Every plan, from £49 a month + VAT per venue. A table-reservation deposit is different: table reservations and floor plans are on Complete Plus at £199 a month + VAT. The function-room deposit stays on every plan.

Can I charge hire, a minimum spend, or per person?

Yes. You can charge room hire, a minimum spend, a price per person, or a mix, and add extras such as coffee, a buffet or a screen, per person or per booking. Each layout has its own capacity.

Is a function room the same as a bedroom?

No. Bedrooms are room management: a diary, housekeeping and the room bill. A function room, boardroom or snug is booked as a whole, by the session or by the hour. The Crown & Anchor is the pilot for Spaces.
