When Lightspeed fits.

Updated 30 October 2026.

Quick answer. Lightspeed Restaurant publishes its tiers and the fee for an extra register, which many till companies do not. On 25 August 2026 those tiers were £79, £149 and £219, and an extra register was £39 a month. They do not state whether that includes VAT. Choose Lightspeed for a food-led restaurant that wants their tableside and stock, and will buy rota and payroll elsewhere. Choose HospitalityApp if those need to be in the same system as the till, and you want to keep your own card machine. Our till is £149 a month + VAT on Complete.

What Lightspeed publishes.

On 25 August 2026 Lightspeed Restaurant’s own tiers were Basic at £79 for one licence, Core at £149 for two, and Pro at £219 for three. A licence is a device slot, so on Basic a single till uses the only licence. Extra registers were £39 a month each, ongoing. They deserve credit for printing that fee. A lot of this market hides it.

They do not state whether those prices include VAT. That is a 20% gap on every figure, and we are not going to guess it. Confirm the VAT line in writing.

The card side is the closed door. Their own wording was that Lightspeed “does not integrate with any other payment processor.” The rate itself was not published. The comparison table showed only “Default” and “Custom”. You cannot budget the card line from the website. If integrated card payments on their device are what you want, that lock-in is the price of the integration, and it is a legitimate reason to buy them rather than us.

The contract, in their favour and not.

The length of the term is not on the pricing page. It is set in the order form you sign. It auto-renews for an equal term. Notice is 90 days. Leaving early charges unpaid non-recurring fees, plus all recurring fees for the rest of the term, plus the difference between list price and any discount you were given. That is their published position, and it is worth reading before you sign. Our monthly plans have no minimum term and no exit fee. Annual is twelve months prepaid and non-refundable. Different shapes. Pick the one you mean.

Bring-your-own hardware is qualified. Their line was that in some cases you might be able to use existing hardware. It is not a yes. Our till runs on an iPad, an Android tablet, or a Windows 10 or 11 PC you already own, including a touchscreen EPOS terminal, with no per-terminal software fee.

Rota, payroll, and the kitchen.

The feature-by-tier table we read had no row for rota, scheduling, payroll or compliance at any tier. Clock-in exists at the till. If you want a rota, a payroll pack and food-safety records, Lightspeed’s own pages send you somewhere else for them. That is a coherent product. It is a till and a restaurant system, not an all-in staff system. For a food-led restaurant or gastropub that will buy rota and payroll from a specialist, Lightspeed can be the right till. Tableside, kitchen display, QR ordering and inventory were paid add-ons, and the prices for those add-ons were not on the page. Ask for them in the quote. The headline tier is not the configured price.

HospitalityApp puts the rota and clock-in on Essentials at £49 a month + VAT, payroll reports on Professional at £99, and the till, stock and printed kitchen tickets on Complete at £149. The kitchen display screen, mobile ordering, table reservations and floor plans are on Complete Plus at £199. We do not run payroll or file RTI. We do not take the card on the till. Compliance records are in the product, with basic checklists on Essentials and the full suite on Professional.

A second till is £0 with us and £39 a month with Lightspeed, on the figure recorded in August 2026. Square and Kobas also charge nothing for an extra device, so this is not unique to us. It is still the line that changes a two-bar pub.

Questions people ask.

How much is Lightspeed Restaurant?

On 25 August 2026 the published tiers were £79 a month for one licence, £149 for two and £219 for three. An extra register was £39 a month. They do not state whether VAT is included. Add-on prices for tableside, kitchen display, QR ordering and inventory were not published. Ask for the configured total.

Can I use my own card processor with Lightspeed?

Their published position is that they do not integrate with any other payment processor. HospitalityApp is the other way round: we do not integrate a card payment at all, so you keep your own terminal and your own rate.

Does Lightspeed include a rota?

Not on the tier table we read. There was no row for rota, scheduling, payroll or compliance. Clock-in exists at the till. HospitalityApp includes the rota from £49 a month + VAT and payroll reports from £99.

Should a small pub buy Lightspeed or HospitalityApp?

Buy Lightspeed if you want their restaurant tools and integrated card payments, and you will run staff elsewhere. Buy HospitalityApp if the till needs to sit with the rota, compliance and a payroll pack, at £149 a month + VAT for the till, and you will keep your own card machine. If you need tap-to-pay on the till, we are the wrong product.
