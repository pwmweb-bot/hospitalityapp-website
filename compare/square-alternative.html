When Square is the better buy.

Updated 30 October 2026.

Quick answer. Square is a strong, published-price till, and for a small café or bar it is often the right one. Free is £0, Plus was £69 per location when we checked on 25 August 2026, extra devices were £0, and in-person UK cards were 1.75%. Choose Square if you need tap-to-pay on the till. Choose HospitalityApp if you want the till, rota, clock-in, payroll reports and compliance in one system, and you want to keep your own card machine. Our till is on Complete, £149 a month + VAT per venue.

The full comparison Our pricing

Square is good at this.

Square publishes a price, in pounds, without a sales call. On 25 August 2026 the restaurants plan was Free at £0 and Plus at £69 per location. A second till added nothing. There is no minimum term: their own line was “No long-term contract required”, with a 30-day trial on Plus. The till runs on an iPad you already own. For a new or small owner-operated café, bar or coffee shop, that is a genuine way to be trading this afternoon.

They also publish the card rate. In person, UK cards were 1.75%. Online was 1.4% plus 25p. Keyed was 2.5%. You can budget the card line. A lot of till companies will not let you do that. We say so in the longer comparison, which dates every figure.

One conflict on their own site, same day: the general UK pricing page showed Plus at “£29+”, and the restaurants page said £69 per location. Both cannot be your price. Ask which page applies to a pub.

Where the cost actually sits.

The software fee is not the large number if the card rate is fixed and you cannot leave it without leaving the till. Square’s payments and the till are the same product. There is no published route to a third-party acquirer. Changing bank means changing till.

Whether that matters is arithmetic. A café taking £8,000 a month on cards is unlikely to beat 1.75%, and the convenience can be worth more than the margin. A pub taking £60,000 a month is paying roughly £1,050 a month in processing at that rate. That sum is against your takings, not against our software fee. Do it before you read “free software” as “cheaper”.

The advertised software price is also not always the working price. Shifts, their rota, was free to five staff and then from £3 a head a month. They do not offer a first-party UK payroll. They point to third parties. MarketMan inventory was £79, a kiosk £35 a device, and loyalty from £25, on the pages we read. Our arithmetic for a realistic single site was Plus at £69, plus Shifts for 12 staff at £21, plus MarketMan at £79, which is from £169 a month before VAT. Check those add-ons on the day. They move.

The kitchen display is a separate limit. Square’s KDS is Android only. Their help centre has told existing iPad users they must migrate hardware. An all-iPad venue that wants a kitchen screen has to buy one that is not an iPad.

Where HospitalityApp fits instead.

Our till is not the cheapest till-only price, and it would be a bad recommendation if a till is all you want. It lives on Complete at £149 a month + VAT per venue. Essentials is £49 and Professional is £99, and neither includes the till. Complete Plus is £199 and adds mobile ordering, the kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans. A second till is £0 in software. Monthly plans have no minimum term. A 30-day free trial of Complete Plus needs no card.

What you get in the same system, rather than as a per-head add-on, is the rota, clock-in by GPS, QR or NFC, payroll reports and a CSV, and compliance records. We do not run payroll or file RTI. We also do not take the card payment. You keep your own acquirer and your own rate. That is the point of difference, and it is also why we cannot offer tap-to-pay on the device that rang the sale. If that tap matters to how you trade, buy Square.

Pubs with rooms can put bar and food on the guest’s room bill. The diary is on every plan, from £49 a month + VAT. The charge from the till needs Complete. Event tickets, with a pass for Apple Wallet and Google Wallet and no ticket fee from us, are on every plan. Loyalty, scanned at the till, is on every plan and off until you switch it on.

Questions people ask.

Is Square cheaper than HospitalityApp?

For till software alone, usually yes. Free is £0 and Plus was £69 per location on 25 August 2026, against our till at £149 a month + VAT on Complete. The card rate is the other bill. At 1.75% in person, a pub taking £60,000 a month on cards pays roughly £1,050 a month in processing, and cannot shop that rate without changing till.

Can I keep my own card machine with Square?

No published route. Square’s payments and the till are the same product. With HospitalityApp you keep your own acquirer, because we do not take the payment on the till. If you need tap-to-pay on that device, Square is the better buy.

Does Square include a rota?

Shifts does. It was free for five staff, then from £3 a head a month. There is no first-party UK payroll. HospitalityApp includes the rota from £49 a month + VAT and payroll reports from £99. We do not run payroll or file RTI.

Are these Square prices still current?

They were read on Square’s own UK pages on 25 August 2026, including a conflict between “£29+” and £69 for Plus. Check the live restaurants page and the general pricing page before you budget. If a figure here is wrong, tell us and we will correct it.
