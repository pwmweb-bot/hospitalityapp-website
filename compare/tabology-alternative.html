When Tabology fits.

Updated 30 October 2026.

Quick answer. Tabology is one of the few UK pub tills that publishes the extra-device prices, not just the headline. On 25 August 2026 the tiers were £39, £59 and £99, and a second till was another £39 a month. They do not state whether VAT is included. Choose Tabology for a one- or two-till wet-led pub that owns an iPad and wants a published, walk-away price. Choose HospitalityApp if you also want payroll reports and compliance records in the same system. Our till is £149 a month + VAT on Complete, and a second till is £0.

The full comparison Our pricing

Tabology is unusually clear.

For a single-site independent pub, Tabology is the closest like-for-like till we looked at, and one of the most transparent. On 25 August 2026 it published all three tiers and the per-device extras. Essentials was £39, Pro £59, Partner £99. An additional terminal was £39 a month, the same as the whole entry plan. Mini EPOS on an iPhone was £19. A kitchen display screen was £11. An iPad menu was £14. They do not state whether any of that includes VAT. Say so when you compare it with a price that is explicitly plus VAT. Ours is plus VAT.

That transparency is also the useful warning. The £39 headline is a one-till price. A pub that wants a till and a rota starts at Pro, £59, because staff scheduling is not in the £39 tier. A two-bar pub with one kitchen screen was, on our arithmetic, Pro at £59 plus a second till at £39 plus the screen at £11, which is £109 a month before the VAT question is settled. Check the live pricing page. The shape of the extras matters more than any one number.

Hardware can be yours. Their line is that you can convert any iPad. A starter pack was £357, or £734.50 with an iPad. Bring-your-own is explicit, which is a fair way to sell a till. A setup or onboarding fee was not published. “Full onboarding” appeared at the £99 tier. Worth asking what the £39 and £59 tiers include on day one.

Cards, contract, and what is not in the box.

Tabology is not the acquirer. You keep your own card machine. The card rate is not published, because it is not their rate. That is the same shape as us, and it is a point in their favour if you already have a merchant deal. We also do not take the payment. We record the method. Your terminal takes the money. Neither of us will tap a card on the till itself. If you need that, Square or Lightspeed is the honest recommendation, and we have pages for both.

The contract line on the website was “We never tie you into a contract.” We could not find a public terms document to read that against, so we report it as a website statement, not as a clause we have checked. Ask for the terms. Our monthly plans have no minimum term, no notice period and no exit fee, and you can cancel in the product. Annual is twelve months prepaid and non-refundable.

Clock-in is included. Payroll is an export, not a payroll run. We did not find a compliance capability. HospitalityApp’s payroll reports, from Professional at £99 a month + VAT, are also not a payroll run. We do not file RTI. The difference we can actually claim is the compliance file: basic checklists on Essentials, the full suite on Professional, in the same app as the rota. If you do not need those records, Tabology’s published device prices are a strong reason to shortlist them.

Questions people ask.

Is Tabology cheaper than HospitalityApp?

On a single till, the published headline is lower. £39 or £59, VAT unstated, against our till at £149 a month + VAT. A second till is £39 a month with Tabology and £0 with us. Add a kitchen screen at £11 and a two-bar pub was £109 a month on our August 2026 arithmetic, before VAT is settled. Compare the configured price, not the entry price.

Does Tabology lock you to their card machine?

No. They are not the acquirer, and the card rate is not theirs to publish. HospitalityApp is the same on that point. Neither product taps the card on the till. If you need that, look at Square or Lightspeed.

Does the £39 plan include a rota?

Not on the pages we read. Staff scheduling starts on Pro at £59. Clock-in is included. Payroll is an export. We did not find compliance records. HospitalityApp includes the rota from £49 a month + VAT.

Are the Tabology prices still right?

They were read on Tabology’s own pricing page on 25 August 2026. They do not state VAT. The “no contract” line is a website statement. We did not find public terms. Check the live page, and tell us if we should correct a figure.
