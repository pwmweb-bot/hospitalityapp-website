Why hotels choose us

The four hotel problems we solve.

Housekeeping by the room.

Allocate rooms by attendant, mark stayover vs check-out, photograph maintenance issues straight to the duty manager. The board updates as rooms are released.

Night audit, ticked off.

Lock-up checklist, fire-panel reading, alarm-set evidence, day-end reconciliation note. Photo-required steps can't be ticked without the picture, and the morning view shows exactly what the night auditor completed and what they didn't.

Breakfast + dinner service rota.

Split shifts handled. Kitchen, breakfast cook, FOH server, porter — all on the same template. Re-use last Saturday's roster with one tap.

Department-level labour cost.

Each department reports its own labour-cost %: rooms division, F&B, admin. See which one slips and pull the lever before payroll closes.

FAQ

Hotel-specific questions.

Do you integrate with our PMS (Mews, Cloudbeds, Little Hotelier)?

Not in v1 — we deliberately stay PMS-agnostic so the rota and compliance side works for any independent. Occupancy is entered as a daily figure for labour-cost calc. Direct PMS sync is on the roadmap.

Can different departments see different things?

Yes. Role chips control which categories and rota cells a team member sees — housekeeping doesn't need bar refusals; the night auditor doesn't need allergen matrices.

What about multi-property groups?

The Multi-site plan gives you central admin across two or more hotels with per-property compliance, rota and labour-cost dashboards in one view.
