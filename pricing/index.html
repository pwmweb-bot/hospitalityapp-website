WHAT YOU’RE SIGNING

No minimum term.
Cancel yourself, any time.

We publish comparison pages that hold other vendors to this standard, so here is ours in the same plain terms — including the one place it is not as simple as the headline.

  • Monthly is rolling, with no minimum term. Month to month, no commitment period, no notice period, and no exit or early-termination fee. Cancel in month two if it is not working and you owe nothing further.
  • You cancel it yourself, in a couple of clicks. The billing screen in the app opens your customer portal, where you cancel directly — no phone call, no email, no notice in writing, no retention conversation. Cancelling stops the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, so you keep full access until then rather than being cut off the same day.
  • No setup fee, onboarding fee or installation charge.
  • Per venue, not per device. A second till (tablet or Windows EPOS terminal), a kitchen display or a handheld does not add a licence fee.
  • If we raise the price, you get 30 days’ notice and can cancel in that window without penalty.
  • Annual is the exception, and we would rather say so here than in the small print. An annual plan is twelve months paid up front in exchange for two months’ discount — so it is a twelve-month commitment, and annual payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. Take the discount if you are confident. If you want the freedom to walk away, stay on monthly.

All of this is in our terms of service rather than only on this page, because a promise on a pricing page is marketing and a promise in the terms is binding. Section 6 covers fees and cancellation; section 8 covers termination.

NO GUESSWORK

Before you
choose a plan.

Will I be charged for the trial?

No card is needed to start the 30-day trial. Add payment details if you decide to continue.

Which plan includes the till?

Complete (£149 per venue/month + VAT) includes the till and stock control. Complete Plus (£199 per venue/month + VAT) adds mobile ordering, kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans. There is no separate per-terminal licence fee.

What is the difference between Complete and Complete Plus?

Complete covers the till, stock, tips, insights and branding. For £50 more per venue/month + VAT, Complete Plus adds mobile ordering, kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans. Complete includes 30 active staff; Complete Plus includes 50.

What does annual billing mean?

You pay for twelve months upfront: £490 for Ticketing, £490 for Essentials, £990 for Professional, £1,490 for Complete or £1,990 for Complete Plus, per venue + VAT. Annual payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. Choose monthly if you want more flexibility.

How do I cancel?

Cancel through the billing portal in the app. Cancellation stops your next renewal, and you retain access for the period already paid for. Read the full terms.

What counts as a venue?

A venue is one physical location. Staff allowances and subscription prices apply per venue. Deactivated staff accounts do not count towards the active-staff allowance.

How much is room management?

Easy rooms, with bar and food on the guest's bill, are not a line on the plan prices above. See room management, then book a 15-minute demo and talk to us. We will not guess a price here.
