NO GUESSWORK

Before you
choose a plan.

Will I be charged for the trial?

No card is needed to start the 30-day trial. Add payment details if you decide to continue.

Which plan includes the till?

Complete (£149 per venue/month + VAT) includes the till and stock control. Complete Plus (£199 per venue/month + VAT) adds mobile ordering, kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans. There is no separate per-terminal licence fee.

What is the difference between Complete and Complete Plus?

Complete covers the till, stock, tips, insights and branding. For £50 more per venue/month + VAT, Complete Plus adds mobile ordering, kitchen display, table reservations and floor plans. Both include 30 active staff.

What does annual billing mean?

You pay for twelve months upfront: £490 for Essentials, £990 for Professional, £1,490 for Complete or £1,990 for Complete Plus, per venue + VAT. Annual payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. Choose monthly if you want more flexibility.

How do I cancel?

Cancel through the billing portal in the app. Cancellation stops your next renewal, and you retain access for the period already paid for. Read the full terms.

What counts as a venue?

A venue is one physical location. Staff allowances and subscription prices apply per venue. Deactivated staff accounts do not count towards the active-staff allowance.
