Account

Delete your account.

How you close your account depends on whether you're on a venue's team, own a venue, or neither. If you've worked at a venue, it keeps the staff and payroll records it needs as your employer.

How to delete your account

If you're on a venue's team

Your account is part of that venue's team, so it can't be deleted from the app. Ask your manager if you want it removed. Once the venue has recorded that you've left, you can delete it yourself as below.

If you own a venue

Owners close their account with us, so the venue is handed over or closed properly first. Email hello@hospitalityapp.co.uk from the address you signed up with (see Owners below).

Everyone else

If you've left the venue you worked at, or signed up and never set up a venue:

  1. In the app, sign in and tap Delete account at the foot of the screen. Your login and profile are deleted straight away.
  2. Or, if you can't use the app, email us at hello@hospitalityapp.co.uk from the address on your account. We confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 30.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account in the app:

  • Your login and your profile
  • Your notification settings and push notification tokens
  • From your staff record at any venue you worked at: your bank details, address, phone number, emergency contact and email

If your account is closed any other way (by us after an email, or while you're still on a venue's team), your login and profile are deleted and your email address is removed from your staff record. The venue keeps the rest of that record, your bank details, address and contact details included, as your employer.

What's retained, and why

Any venue you worked at keeps the rest of your staff record, which its payroll and right-to-work records need: your name, NI number, date of birth, start and leaving dates, pay rate, tax code, position, till PIN and its right-to-work check, and every shift, hour and pay record. That record is your employer's, kept because UK law and its statutory obligations require it, for example:

  • Shift history & clock-in logs — typically 2 years (Working Time Regulations 1998)
  • Payroll records — 6 years from end of tax year (HMRC)
  • Food-safety / HACCP entries you signed off — kept for the inspection period required by your venue's food authority
  • Alcohol-licensing logs (refusals, training) where you were the named recorder
  • Compliance evidence photos you uploaded — retained per the relevant compliance category's renewal cycle

After the legal retention period expires, we delete or further anonymise the remaining data. If you have specific questions about what's retained for your account, email us and we'll detail it.

Owners

If you're the venue owner and want to delete the entire business (including all staff, rotas, compliance and event records), email us with the subject line "Business deletion request". We confirm identity, notify your team, and wipe everything within 30 days.
