1 Register

Set up your account and this card.

This first step gets you an account, your venue, and links the card in your hand to it as your clock-in tag.

  • Download the app and create your free account — your name, email and a password, then confirm the email we send you.

How your team clocks in

Stick it on the wall. Tap to clock in.

Once the card is registered, this is the whole routine for staff — three taps, no paper.

STEP 1

Put the card on the wall.

By the door, behind the bar, at the staff entrance — wherever staff pass. Keep it within easy phone reach and off large metal surfaces.

STEP 2

Open the app, tap "Clock in".

Staff open HospitalityApp on their own phone and tap Clock in. The card doesn't launch anything on its own — the app leads.

STEP 3

Hold the phone to the card.

The app reads the tag, confirms they're at the venue, and logs the time. Same routine to clock out. That's it.

FAQ

Activation questions.

Do I just tap the card to clock in?

Not on its own — the card is a tag the app reads, not a button. Staff open the app, tap "Clock in", then hold their phone to the card. It's three taps and takes a couple of seconds.

What if my phone won't scan the card during setup?

Most modern phones read it instantly held flat against the card. If yours won't, you can type the tag's ID into Business → Clock-in instead, or drop us a line at hello@hospitalityapp.co.uk and we'll help.

Can I use more than one card?

Yes — register as many tags as you like, one per door or till. Each one is added the same way in Business → Clock-in.

What if someone forgets to clock out?

Any shift still open past its scheduled end flags to the duty manager, who can fix the clock-out from the inbox. Every edit is logged with who changed it and when, so the record stays clean.

What does it cost?

You start on a 30-day free trial — no card details needed to set up and try it. When you're ready, plans start from £49 per venue per month. See full pricing.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored in EU-region infrastructure, isolated per business, GDPR-compliant by default. You can export or delete everything any time.
