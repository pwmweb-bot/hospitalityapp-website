What you're signing

No minimum term. Cancel yourself, any time.

We publish comparison pages that hold other vendors to this standard, so here is ours in the same plain terms — including the one place it is not as simple as the headline.

  • Monthly is rolling, with no minimum term. Month to month, no commitment period, no notice period, and no exit or early-termination fee. Cancel in month two if it is not working and you owe nothing further.
  • You cancel it yourself, in a couple of clicks. The billing screen in the app opens your customer portal, where you cancel directly — no phone call, no email, no notice in writing, no retention conversation. Cancelling stops the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, so you keep full access until then rather than being cut off the same day.
  • No setup fee, onboarding fee or installation charge.
  • Per venue, not per device. A second till, a kitchen display or a handheld does not add a licence fee.
  • If we raise the price, you get 30 days' notice and can cancel in that window without penalty.
  • Annual is the exception, and we would rather say so here than in the small print. An annual plan is twelve months paid up front in exchange for two months' discount — so it is a twelve-month commitment, and annual payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. Take the discount if you are confident. If you want the freedom to walk away, stay on monthly.

All of this is in our terms of service rather than only on this page, because a promise on a pricing page is marketing and a promise in the terms is binding. Section 6 covers fees and cancellation; section 8 covers termination.

FAQ

Pricing questions.

What does "per venue" mean?

One physical site under one licence. A pub with one address is one venue. A small group with three sites pays per venue, with volume discounts at five and ten venues.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No — the 30-day trial is free and needs no card to start. Sign up and use everything straight away. If you decide to stay, you add payment details at the end; if you don't, the trial simply ends. Nothing is ever charged automatically.

Which features are available during the trial?

Every trial starts on Complete (the full Venue OS) so you can feel the till, kitchen display and full compliance suite. At the end of the trial we downgrade you to the plan you chose at signup — or to Complete if you don't pick one.

How do I get the till / POS?

The till, kitchen display and stock control come with the Professional plan (£99/venue/mo), along with gross-profit margins. On Essentials and want the till? Just upgrade to Professional — there's no separate add-on to buy, and no per-terminal fee, so you can run the till on as many tablets as you like.

What happens above the included staff allowance?

Each plan includes an active-staff allowance — Essentials 10, Professional 20, Complete 30. "Active staff" means team members with an active account on your venue (deactivated leavers don't count). Per-staff overage above the allowance is published at +£3 per extra staff member per month on every plan, but is not auto-billed today — if your venue grows past the allowance we'll talk to you about it at renewal, not mid-cycle. Staff use the app for free.

Can I pay annually?

Yes. Annual billing gives you two months free — Essentials £490/yr, Professional £990/yr, Complete £1,490/yr. Switch at any renewal.

Do you offer multi-venue or group discounts?

Yes. Five or more venues = 15% off the per-venue rate. Ten or more = 25% off. Multi-venue customers also get cross-venue dashboards and central admin included.

VAT?

Prices shown are excluding VAT. UK VAT-registered businesses can reclaim through the usual route.

Is there a minimum contract?

Not on a monthly plan. Monthly is rolling month to month — no minimum term, no notice period, no exit or early-termination fee. Cancel in month two and you owe nothing further.

Annual is the exception. An annual plan is twelve months paid in advance in exchange for two months' discount, so it is a twelve-month commitment and annual payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. If you want the freedom to leave at any point, stay on monthly. Both are set out in section 6 of our terms, not just here.

How do I cancel?

You do it yourself. The billing screen in the app opens your customer portal, where cancellation is a couple of clicks — no phone call, no email, no written notice, no retention conversation. Cancelling stops the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, so you keep full access until then. If you would rather we did it, email us and we will.

What happens if you put the price up?

You get 30 days' notice, and you can cancel within that window without penalty. It is in section 6 of our terms.

What about my data if I cancel?

You can export everything (staff, rota, compliance logs, takings, till sales) at any time. After cancellation we keep the data for 30 days in case you change your mind, then delete it.
