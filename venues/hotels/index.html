WHY VENUES LIKE YOURS CHOOSE US

The four hotel problems
we solve.

01

Housekeeping, by the room.

Allocate rooms by attendant, mark stayover against check-out, and photograph maintenance issues straight to the duty manager. The board updates as rooms are released.

02

The night audit, ticked off.

Lock-up checklist, fire-panel reading, alarm-set evidence, day-end reconciliation note. Photo-required steps cannot be ticked without the picture, and the morning view shows exactly what the night auditor completed and what they did not.

03

Breakfast and dinner on one rota.

Split shifts handled. Kitchen, breakfast cook, front-of-house server and porter all on the same template, and last Saturday’s roster re-used with one tap.

04

Labour cost by department.

Rooms division, food and beverage, and admin each report their own labour cost percentage. See which one slips and pull the lever before payroll closes.

COMPLIANCE CATEGORIES

What inspectors and OTAs
both want.

From the EHO to the booking sites to the fire-risk assessor — one source of truth, photo-evidenced and exportable.

  • Food safety HACCP, breakfast service logs, allergen sign-offs, room-service food handling.
  • Fire safety Weekly alarm test, monthly emergency lighting, fire-door inspection log, evacuation drill record.
  • Health & safety Risk assessments, COSHH for cleaning chemicals, Legionella checks, lift inspection date.
  • Housekeeping Room-checked sign-off, linen temperature log, deep-clean rotation, lost property register.
  • Workforce Right to work, contracts, training records, personal licence for bar staff.
  • Insurance & licences Public liability, employers’ liability, premises licence, music licence — with renewal alerts.
