WHY VENUES LIKE YOURS CHOOSE US

The four pub problems
we solve.

01

Cellar checks, photographed.

Build the cellar log you actually keep — daily line clean, gas levels, cellar temperature — each with a photo trigger that blocks submission until the evidence is attached. The brewery rep walks in, you tap export, done.

02

Alcohol licensing on file.

Log refusals, track DPS changes, and store your premises licence and summary as photo evidence. Renewal-tracked entries alert you before they lapse.

03

Late-night refreshment ready.

A closing-shift checklist that locks before you close: door staff log, last orders called, kitchen off, alarm armed. No more wondering whether anyone put the bins out.

04

The sports-fixture rota.

Build a Six Nations Saturday template once — double-handed bar, glass collector, door staff — and drop it onto every fixture day for the season.

COMPLIANCE CATEGORIES

What inspectors
will ask for.

Every category is in the app. You do not need a separate binder.

  • Food safety HACCP records, fridge temperatures, allergen sign-offs, rodent log.
  • Fire safety Weekly alarm test, monthly emergency lighting, extinguisher dates.
  • Alcohol licensing Refusal log, age-verification policy, DPS notice, premises licence.
  • Health & safety Risk assessments, accident book, first aid, COSHH.
  • Workforce Right to work, contracts, training records, BII certifications.
  • Insurance Public liability, employers’ liability, buildings — with renewal alerts.
