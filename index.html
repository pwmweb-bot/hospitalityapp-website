BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few good
questions.

What can I try for free?

You get the Complete feature set for 30 days, including the till, stock control and the full compliance suite. Choose your plan after you’ve explored the app.

How do I get my team started?

Create your venue, invite your team by email and start building your rota. Staff can join from their phones. Existing rotas can be imported on request.

Does it work across multiple venues?

Yes. Manage venues from one login, with group views for labour, sales and compliance, plus central menu and pricing controls.

Do we need special devices?

The app works on iOS, Android and the web. The till supports tablets and compatible Windows EPOS terminals. See till details.
