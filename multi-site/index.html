Can venues keep different menus and prices?

Yes. Use a shared menu, venue-specific price overrides or independent menus managed centrally.

Who can see each venue?

Access is based on role and venue. Staff, venue managers, area managers and head office can have views appropriate to their responsibilities.

Do we have to launch every venue at once?

No. Start with one location, then bring more sites into the system as you are ready. Discuss your rollout with the team.
