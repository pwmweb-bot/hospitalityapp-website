WHY WE EXIST

Make the everyday
work together.

A rota here. A checklist there. Another system for the till.

Hospitality App brings those everyday jobs into one product. The aim is straightforward: clearer information for the team, and fewer loose ends for the person running the venue.

Start with the tools your venue needs, with room to add more as your operation grows.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Four things
we build against.

  • One source of truth. Hours worked feed payroll. Compliance checks attach to shifts. The rota cross-checks itself against leave. Enter it once.
  • Built for owner-operators. The defaults assume a manager who is also on the floor — fast to set up, quick to run, no implementation consultant required.
  • Honest pricing. Per venue, from £49 a month + VAT. No per-terminal fees, no per-user fees, and no surprise add-ons to unlock the basics.
  • Your data is yours. Operational data is stored in the EU with row-level security per business, and you can export or delete it at any time. This marketing site sets no cookies and our analytics are cookie-free.

RUN BY REAL VENUES

Every feature survives
a real Friday night.

Hospitality App is not a mockup. Our first venue, The Foregate, runs its rota, clock-in, compliance records and payroll on it day to day — which is exactly how we want to build: against the messy reality of a working venue, rather than a slide deck.

It is an early-stage product in active development. The roadmap is shaped by the venues using it, and feedback moves into the product quickly.

The product runs on iOS, Android and the web, and the till runs on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) too — on a touchscreen EPOS terminal or an ordinary Windows PC. Whether you run one venue or a whole group, email us; every message reaches a person.
