This guide takes you through adding a function room, from its name and capacity to the price guests see. You can save at any step and come back later.

Before you start, open **Spaces**. If you want guests to pay a deposit by card, [connect card payments](/help/settings/connect-card-payments/) first.

## Add the space

### 1 Name it and set the size

Go to **Spaces** and choose **New space**. Give it the name guests will recognise, such as “The Function Room”, and set the most people it can hold.

![Spaces list for The Crown & Anchor, with the Function Room, the Snug and the Boardroom. Example data.](/assets/help/spaces-list.png)

The Spaces list shows each room, whether online booking is on, and the price guests are offered.

### 2 Choose the layouts

Pick the layouts you offer, such as theatre, boardroom or banquet. Each layout has its own capacity, so a guest booking a banquet sees the right numbers.

## Decide when it can be booked

### 3 Set sessions or hourly booking

Offer fixed sessions (morning, afternoon, evening), booking by the hour, or both. Add setting-up and clearing time so back-to-back bookings do not clash. The detail is in [Sessions, prices and deposits](/help/spaces/sessions-prices-and-deposits/).

## Set the price

### 4 Choose how you charge

Charge room hire, a minimum spend, a price per person, or a mix. Ask for a deposit or full payment when the guest books, or take payment on the day.

### 5 Add extras

Add things guests can order with the room: teas and coffees, a buffet, a screen. Price each one per person or per booking. See [Adding extras](/help/spaces/adding-extras/).

## Check and publish

### 6 Review and save

Check the summary, then save. The space appears in your Spaces list straight away. If online booking is on, guests can book it from your booking page.

> Tip: read the confirmation before you save, so you know what the guest will receive.
