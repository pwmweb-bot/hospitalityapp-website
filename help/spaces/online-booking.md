Online booking lets a guest choose a space, a date and a session without calling the venue. Each space can be on or off.

## Turn it on

On **Spaces**, the list shows **Online booking** for the venue, and each space shows whether guests can book it. Turn it on for the rooms you want on the public page.

![The Spaces list, with online booking on, for The Crown & Anchor. Example data.](/assets/help/spaces-list.png)

Guests then see the spaces you have published, the party size, and only the sessions that are free.

![The guest’s first step, choosing a space and a party size. Example data.](/assets/help/spaces-guest-pick.png)

## Payments

If a space asks for a deposit or the full amount, [connect card payments](/help/settings/connect-card-payments/) first. The money goes to your own Stripe account. Hospitality App takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply.

A space set to [enquiries](/help/spaces/taking-an-enquiry/) still appears on the page. The guest sends a request instead of paying.

Link the page from your website wherever you want guests to book a room. Dates you have [blocked](/help/spaces/blocking-dates/) are not offered.
