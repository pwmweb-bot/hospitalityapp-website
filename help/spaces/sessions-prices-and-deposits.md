A space can be booked in sessions, by the hour, or both. The price can be room hire, a minimum spend, a price per person, or a mix.

## Sessions

A session is a named block of time, such as morning, afternoon or evening, with a start and an end. Guests only see sessions that are still free.

![Choosing a date and a session for the Snug. Morning is unavailable. Afternoon and evening show a price. Example data for The Crown & Anchor.](/assets/help/spaces-guest-when.png)

On that screen the guest picks the date, then a session. A session that is already taken is not offered.

If you also allow booking by the hour, set the hours the room can be used and any setting-up or clearing time that must stay free. That gap stops one booking running into the next.

## How the price is worked out

You can charge in three ways, and you can combine them:

- **Room hire**, a set amount for the session.
- **Minimum spend**, an amount that must be spent, with anything above it still to pay.
- **Per person**, a price multiplied by the number of guests.

The guest sees the total before they pay, including any [extras](/help/spaces/adding-extras/).

## The deposit

When the guest books, you can take:

- no payment now, and settle on the day
- a deposit by card
- the full amount by card

The card payment goes to your own Stripe account. Hospitality App takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. Connect payments first, in [Connect card payments](/help/settings/connect-card-payments/).

![The guest’s summary for an afternoon session, with extras and a deposit still to pay. Example data.](/assets/help/spaces-guest-pay.png)

A space can instead take an enquiry, which you confirm yourself. That is covered in [Taking an enquiry](/help/spaces/taking-an-enquiry/).
