Block a date when a space should not be booked, even though the room still exists. A wedding you are taking by hand, a refit, or a day the venue is closed are the usual reasons.

## Close the date

Open the space and block the date, or the session, that must stay closed. Guests using [online booking](/help/spaces/online-booking/) are not offered that session. A session that is only blocked for the afternoon can still be booked in the morning.

A block is not a booking. It does not take a deposit and it does not email a guest. When the date is free again, remove the block and the session goes back on sale.

If the room is already booked, [change or cancel that booking](/help/spaces/changing-or-cancelling-a-booking/) first. A block does not move a guest who is already confirmed.
