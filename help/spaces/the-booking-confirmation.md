When a booking is confirmed, the guest sees the space, the date, the session, the party size, what they have paid and what is left to pay.

![A confirmed booking for the Snug on a phone, with the deposit paid and the balance due. Example data.](/assets/help/spaces-phone-confirmed.png)

The same details are in the confirmation email. It uses your venue name. You can add your own note, for arrival instructions or a cancellation line, before the booking is saved.

## If they paid a deposit

The confirmation separates the deposit already paid from the balance due on the day. Extras they added, such as a buffet or a projector, are listed on the same summary. See [Sessions, prices and deposits](/help/spaces/sessions-prices-and-deposits/).

## If it was an enquiry

An enquiry does not send a paid confirmation until you confirm it. Once you do, the guest gets the same confirmation as a booking. See [Taking an enquiry](/help/spaces/taking-an-enquiry/).
