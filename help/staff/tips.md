Tips recorded on the till can be shared out under the Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2023. The longer explanation of the Act is in the [tronc guide](/guides/tronc-tips-act-2023). This is the short version of the buttons.

## What the till already has

Card tips taken on a bill are pulled from the till. Cash tips are entered by hand. A tip can be split at the time of the sale, and a discount does not change the tip. See [Tabs and split bills](/help/till/tabs-and-split-bills/).

## Calculate, review, finalise

1. **Calculate** the share for the period.
2. **Review** the amounts and who they go to.
3. **Finalise** to produce the dated tronc statement the Act requires.

Payroll reports themselves, hours and pay for a date range, are a separate step and are on Professional and above. We produce the report and a CSV. We do not run payroll or file RTI.

A rate change is dated, so shifts before that date keep the old rate.
