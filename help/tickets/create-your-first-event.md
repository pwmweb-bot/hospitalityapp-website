An event is a quiz, a dinner, a tasting or any night you sell places for. Tickets are sold from Hospitality App, and the sales sit in the same reports as the till.

## Start the event

Open **Events** and choose **New event**. The steps are Details, Tickets, Questions, Doors, then a review.

![The events list, with tickets sold and takings for each night. Example data for The Crown & Anchor.](/assets/help/events-list.png)

On **Details**, set the name, the date and a short description. A night can run on past midnight without becoming a second event.

## Tickets, questions, doors

- Add at least one ticket type, with a price and a limit. Free tickets are allowed. See [Ticket types and limits](/help/tickets/ticket-types-and-limits/).
- Add a checkout question if you need dietary notes or a team name. See [Checkout questions](/help/tickets/checkout-questions/).
- Set when the doors open. Staff scan tickets from a phone. See [Scan tickets at the door](/help/tickets/scan-tickets-at-the-door/).

The review step shows the public page before you publish. You can also set the wording of the email the guest receives.

## The money

Paid tickets go to your own Stripe account, including a booking fee you set for the venue and the VAT on that fee. Hospitality App takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. [Connect card payments](/help/settings/connect-card-payments/) first.

Events and tickets are included on every plan. No ticket fee from us.

Food or drink included in the ticket can be rung on the till at £0, so the kitchen and the bar still see the order.
