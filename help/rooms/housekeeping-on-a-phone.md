Open **Rooms** and choose **Housekeeping**, on the web or on a phone. Each room shows its status, who is cleaning it, and whether a guest is arriving early.

The statuses are:

- **Dirty**
- **Cleaning**
- **Clean**
- **Inspected**

Filter the list by status, or show every room. Rooms with an early arrival are marked and listed first. A guest who is still in house is shown on the room, so it is not cleaned while they are there.

Choose **Start cleaning** when the room is begun. When something is wrong, use **Report a problem** and attach a photo. Repairs, with a priority and any out-of-order dates, are logged under [Maintenance](/help/rooms/logging-a-repair/).
