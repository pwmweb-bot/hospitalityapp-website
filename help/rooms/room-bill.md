Bar and food can go straight onto the guest’s room bill. There is no paper chit to carry upstairs.

## From the till

1. Take the order at the bar, or at the table on a staff phone. It sits on an open tab, the same as any other sale. See [Take your first order](/help/till/take-your-first-order/).
2. From that open tab, charge it to the guest’s room.
3. The order lands on that stay, next to the room nights.

## At check-out

The guest pays whatever is left on the bill, by card, cash or bank transfer. A deposit, or the room charge itself, may already have been paid online when the stay was [booked](/help/rooms/booking-a-stay/).

The invoice is emailed. It is itemised. Room nights are at 20% VAT. The pint and the pie keep the VAT that was already on the till.

## If room management is off

The charge-to-room choice only appears where rooms are switched on for the venue. A tab that is not charged to a room is paid in the usual way, on the till. The till itself does not take the card. Your card machine does.
