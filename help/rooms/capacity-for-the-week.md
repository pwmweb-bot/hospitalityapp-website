Open **Rooms** and choose **Capacity**. It compares rooms sold with rooms available, and shows guests, the average room rate (ADR) and revenue per available room (RevPAR).

Use it for the week or the month. A heat map shows which nights and room types are filling up, so a quiet Tuesday and a full Saturday are visible without opening each stay.

The percentage along the top of the [diary](/help/rooms/using-the-room-diary/) is the same idea for a single night: rooms occupied divided by rooms you can sell. Capacity is the longer view, with the rate and the revenue alongside.
