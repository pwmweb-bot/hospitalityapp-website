Open **Rooms** and choose **Maintenance**. Each issue shows its priority, who is on it, and any dates the room is out of order.

Log the repair, set the priority and name the person dealing with it. If the room cannot be let, set the out-of-order dates. Only those nights are blocked. The rest of the diary can still be booked.

A blocked or out-of-order night is shown on the [diary](/help/rooms/using-the-room-diary/) and cannot take a stay. A problem spotted while cleaning, including one with a photo, is reported from [housekeeping](/help/rooms/housekeeping-on-a-phone/).
