A tab is an open bill with a name. A split is that bill divided by item. Both happen on the same till.

## A tab

Save the open sale against a name. The tab list shows how many are open, what is outstanding, and what has been taken today. Bring a tab back to add another round, then take payment or [charge it to a room](/help/rooms/room-bill/).

Tables work the same way from the floor plan: covers, the open bill, and pay at that table. You can hand the tablet over for the guest to pay, or send a payment link. The card is still taken by your card machine, or by the link, not by a processor built into the till.

## Split by item

Split the bill by item when people are paying separately. Move the lines onto each person’s bill. Each one is paid on its own.

## Tips and discounts

A tip can be card or cash, and a tip can be split. A discount never changes the tip. A price change in pounds asks for a reason.

Staff drinks have their own button and are recorded against a person. A refund, of one item or the whole sale, is for a manager. It can be cash, card or Apple Pay. A refund receipt prints, and the reason is recorded.

When the bill is done, print it, reprint it, or email it. The receipt can carry your logo.
