Five steps cover the first afternoon. Do them in order. Each one links to the short guide.

1. **Card payments, if guests will pay you online.** [Connect card payments](/help/settings/connect-card-payments/) so a deposit or a ticket goes to your own Stripe account. Skip this if you only need the rota and the till.
2. **The people who run it.** [Add a manager](/help/settings/add-a-manager/), then anyone else who needs to clock in.
3. **Next week’s shifts.** [Build the rota](/help/staff/build-next-weeks-rota/) and publish it. The team clock in against it.
4. **The till.** [Take a first order](/help/till/take-your-first-order/), then [close the day](/help/till/close-the-till/) once so the Z is familiar before a Saturday.
5. **The part you sell on top.** A function room is [Spaces](/help/spaces/setting-up-a-function-room/). Bedrooms are [Rooms](/help/rooms/setting-up-rooms/). A quiz or a dinner is [Tickets](/help/tickets/create-your-first-event/).

The same guides open in the app from the ? button, on the screen they belong to.

If a step does not match what you see, [contact the team](/contact/) and name the screen you were on.
