The people who work for you each have a record. A manager can run the rota, the till settings and the pay reports. A member of staff sees their own shifts, clock-in and messages.

## Add the person

Add them to the team with their name and the venue they work at. Someone who works at more than one venue can have a rate at each. Their shifts still cannot overlap.

Set what they are allowed to do. Giving someone manager access is what lets them publish a rota, refund a sale, and open pay reports. Leave that off for someone who only needs to clock in and read the rota.

## Pay details

Hourly rate, National Insurance, tax code and bank details live on the record. They are restricted in the database, not only hidden on screen. A change to bank details alerts managers.

A rate change is dated. Shifts before the change keep the old rate, which is what the payroll report uses.

Staff added here are the people you put on [next week’s rota](/help/staff/build-next-weeks-rota/).
