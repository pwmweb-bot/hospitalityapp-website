WHAT IT DOES

What goes into
the payroll run.

  • A payroll report for any date range — hand a bookkeeper a finished pack instead of a shoebox.
  • Payment basis — planned hours, exact minutes, or rounded to fifteen. Your call, applied consistently.
  • Three views — itemised by person, weekly summary and summary table. Checking one person, signing off a week and exporting are different jobs.
  • Fix clock data inline. A missing clock-out is added in the report itself, with no context switch to another screen.
  • Email the pack — an HTML summary plus an itemised CSV, sent only to a verified payroll address, so a typo cannot leak a wage run.
  • CSV export, summary or itemised, for whatever your accountant actually uses.
  • All-branches payroll report — one report across every site an owner holds, with multi-site staff consolidated at their per-venue rates.
  • “My pay” for staff — day, week or month, scheduled against actual per shift, hours times their rate, in the app. It answers the most common recurring question a manager gets.
  • Off-rota worked time is shown separately rather than folded into a total, so a staff member can see it and a manager has to decide about it.

WHY IT MATTERS

The pay conversation,
when there isn’t one.

The pay-day argument is almost never about the money. It is about the evidence. Was I here at nine or ten? Did the swap go through? Why is Tuesday four hours and not five? When the answer is a screen the staff member has been looking at themselves all fortnight, the argument does not happen. When it is a scribble on a printout, the manager loses.

The verified-recipient rule on the emailed pack is a small guardrail with an outsized effect. Leaked wage runs are a real category of hospitality data breach, and they almost always trace back to a mistyped email address being trusted. Refusing to send until the recipient has clicked once is the difference.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Does Essentials include payroll reports?

Essentials includes rota and attendance. Payroll reports are included in Professional, Complete and Complete Plus.

Will it fit our existing payroll process?

We produce payroll reports and a CSV export; we don’t run payroll or file RTI. Discuss your accountant’s requirements, export needs and any required integrations with the team before changing your process.
