Owner Today

The card that decides whether the week was any good.

Labour cost week-to-date against budget, labour % against target, a Flash P&L card (Sales, Gross profit, Labour), and a "needs you" list of approvals, missed tasks and anything requiring an owner decision. The number that decides whether the week was any good, on the first screen.

Manager Today

Everything the shift needs before service.

Shifts scheduled and unfilled today, approvals waiting, missed tasks, a takings prompt from 14:00 if the day isn't logged, and shortcuts to tronc and the noticeboard. Same app, manager tab — nothing an owner-only tool would gate behind a paywall.

Reports hub

The five owner-level reports.

  • Takings — daily or weekly gross takings, running labour %, forecast vs on-track.
  • Labour — shifts, hours, cost per staff member, planned vs actual vs variance.
  • Payroll — the wages run (see Payroll module →).
  • Tips — the tronc statement (see Tips & tronc module →).
  • Compliance — status across all ten categories, drill in to see overdue.
Till Insights

Five tabs, one honest picture of the venue.

  • Busy periods — busiest slot, peak covers, sales per labour hour; a day × hour revenue heatmap; a sales-vs-staffing chart that answers are we staffed for the rush?
  • Forecast — expected takings and covers for the coming week, by weekday.
  • Labour — labour %, cost, sales per labour hour, day-by-day.
  • Profit — revenue, gross profit, GP%, stock at cost; most profitable and lowest-margin products; waste this period; GP by category; and an explicit "costs missing" warning so a flattering GP figure can't be built on absent data.
  • Trends — 13 months of revenue / GP% / labour%, with same-season comparison.
Why it matters

Speed of decision is the independent's edge.

An independent operator's advantage over a chain is speed of decision, and they usually lose it to not knowing until the accounts arrive six weeks later. When labour % is a live figure, GP is calculated from container costs the till already knows about, and busy periods stare back at you as a heatmap, the decisions get made this week — not next quarter.

The "costs missing" warning is deliberate. A GP figure built on half-populated cost data isn't a GP figure; it's a story. The Profit tab refuses to lie by omission, and the operator gets to see what's true.
