Selling

The order screen.

  • Counter service or table service, set per venue.
  • Category rail, sub-category chips, search — arranged the way the bar actually sells (see Till preview below).
  • Measure popup — Half / Pint, Single / Double, 175ml / 250ml / Bottle. Each measure has its own price and its own pour size, so GP is calculated per pour.
  • Line notes, void line, running subtotal / service charge / total.
  • Add a note that prints on the bill and the kitchen ticket.
Taking money

Payment, tips, splits, tabs, tables.

  • Payment method, tip presets and custom tip.
  • Split evenly between N people, or uneven per-guest splits with per-share amounts and "Paid" badges as each settles.
  • Bar tabs — save a sale against a named tab (mobile or email). The tab list shows Open count, Outstanding, Taken today.
  • Tables — floor plan with Open / To settle / Guests / Taken today, seat covers, a "Food ready" pill, pay per table.
  • Receipts — print, reprint, preview, or email to the customer.
  • Daily takings sync back automatically, so the takings record fills itself. No end-of-night reconciliation.
Control

Attribution, refunds, drawer opens, staff drinks.

  • Lock screen with PIN and idle auto-lock — the next order is attributed to whoever unlocked. Every sale has a name against it.
  • Recent sales — reprint, and cancel & refund with a required reason (manager only). Refunds have a paper trail.
  • Open cash drawer (no-sale) — logged, counted, and reported. The classic shrinkage vector becomes a number on a report.
  • Staff drinks — bank a drink against a person, redeem later. Stops the "put it on my tab" black hole.
  • Void sale / void table bill with confirmation.

Why it matters

What the till changes about running a venue.

Two things separate a hospitality till from a retail till dressed up in beer graphics: the measure popup, and the model of trust. A pint and a half share a product; a single and a double share a spirit; a 175 and a 250 share a wine bottle. If those aren't the same product with different prices, GP and stock depletion are theatre.

And every sale carries the name of the person who took it, because the next order isn't served until someone re-enters their PIN. That's the model that turns cash-drawer opens, refunds and staff drinks from arguments into numbers on a report.

The till is included in Complete. There is no per-terminal fee, no processor lock-in, no "POS bolt-on" — we don't earn a rebate from a card processor, so we don't need to bundle one.
