WHAT IT DOES

A night,
with a door list.

  • Free or paid. A ticket can cost nothing, or it can have a price. You can add a booking fee for your venue, and set the VAT on that fee. The fee is yours.
  • Payments go to your own Stripe account. Hospitality App takes no commission on tickets. Stripe’s own fees still apply.
  • A unique QR, emailed. Every ticket has its own code. The guest gets it by email.
  • The door, on a phone. The scanner checks the ticket in. It flags a code that was already scanned, has been refunded, or belongs to a different event.
  • On your website. Paste a snippet where the tickets should appear. It counts down, live, to the moment they go on sale.
  • Refund the tickets you choose. Those places can go back on sale.
  • Past midnight. A night can run on after midnight without becoming a second event.
  • Included food and drink. Ring it through the till at £0, so the kitchen and the bar still see the order.
  • Not the rota diary. Private parties, fixtures and the function note on the rota stay where they are, under team and communication. This page is for selling the places.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Which plan includes tickets?

Events and tickets are not on the published plan prices. Book a 15-minute demo and we will show how a night sits alongside the till and the rota.

Who receives the money?

Payments go to your own Stripe account, including a booking fee you set for the venue. Hospitality App takes no commission on tickets. Stripe’s own fees still apply.
