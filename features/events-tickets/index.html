Good to know

A few practical answers.

Do I need a separate ticketing site?

No. Events live in Hospitality App, with the sales in the same reports as the till.

Can I limit tickets?

Yes. Set a limit for each ticket type and for the whole event. See Ticket types and limits.

How do tickets get checked?

Scan the QR at the door from a staff phone. Each ticket only counts once. The steps are in Scan tickets at the door.

Can they keep the ticket in a wallet?

From the confirmation email, or the paid page, they can add it to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. That is the product. It does not mean Google’s class review is finished, or that Google Wallet is available to every guest.

Where are the guides?

In the help centre under Tickets, and behind the ? button in the app.

Which plan includes tickets?

Events and tickets are not on the published plan prices. Book a 15-minute demo and we will show how a night sits alongside the till.
