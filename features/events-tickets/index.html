WHAT IT DOES

A night,
with a door list.

  • A public page for the night. Turn on “Sell tickets online” when it should go live. Until then, the page tells people tickets are not on.
  • Ticket types. A name, a price, a VAT rate and a limit. A free ticket is emailed with no card payment.
  • Payments go to your own Stripe account. Connect Stripe before you publish a paid night, or guests will not be able to pay. Hospitality App takes no commission on tickets. Stripe’s own fees still apply.
  • On your website. Paste a snippet where the tickets should appear. It lists what is on, counts down to the on-sale time, and takes the payment. The snippet is only your public address.
  • A QR code on each ticket. The confirmation email includes one for every ticket, and the short code as well. At the door, scan the QR or enter the code.
  • The door list. Door staff can check people in. You can also let them read names and codes without ticking anyone in.
  • Refunds. Refund selected tickets, or the rest of an order. The money goes back to the card, and those seats go back on sale. A free ticket is simply cancelled.
  • Not the rota diary. Private parties, fixtures and the function note on the rota stay where they are, under team and communication. This page is for selling the places.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Which plan includes tickets?

Events and tickets are not on the published plan prices. Book a 15-minute demo and we will show how a night sits alongside the till and the rota.

Who receives the money?

Payments go to your own Stripe account. Hospitality App takes no commission on tickets. Stripe’s own fees still apply, and refunds are handled from the guest list.
