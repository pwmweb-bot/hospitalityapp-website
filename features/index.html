Where each module runs

One codebase, two apps, three kiosks.

The web app at my.hospitalityapp.co.uk is the full product. The native app (iOS + Android) is the staff and manager experience with GPS, NFC, camera and offline-ish speed. The till, kitchen display and mobile ordering pair as full-screen kiosks with a single-use code — no login, ever.

The modules

Pick a spoke, get the whole picture.

Rota & scheduling

Drag-drop builder, four views (role / staff / timeline / week), live labour-cost ticker, over-budget guard on publish, shift templates with task lists, open shifts staff claim from the app.

Time & attendance

Three clock-in methods (GPS, QR, NFC) that combine. Overnight-safe, auto-close sweep, one-tap discrepancies queue so a forgotten clock-out never becomes a 30-hour shift.

Payroll & pay

A pack built from actual clock punches. Planned / exact / rounded basis, three views, verified-recipient email, all-branches run, "My pay" for staff.

Tips & tronc

Card tips from the till, cash tips entered manually, Calculate → Review → Finalise. A dated tronc statement per week — the artefact the 2023 Act requires.

Till / POS

Full-screen, no chrome. Counter or table service, measure popup (half/pint, single/double), tables + tabs, PIN-attributed sales, cash-drawer opens logged. Included in Professional (£99/venue/mo).

Mobile ordering

A server's phone as a roaming till. PIN-unlock model, three menu layouts, orders land at the till and the pass. The cheapest possible extra terminal.

Kitchen display

Full-screen tickets at the pass or filtered to a station. Products carry a station; routing is automatic. Replaces the printer and the spike.

Stock & GP

Container-first stocktake (kegs and bottles counted in quarters, never millilitres). Auto-depletion on sale. Real GP, not a quarterly guess.

Reporting & insights

Owner Today, Manager Today, Reports hub, Till Insights (busy periods, forecast, labour, profit, 13-month trends). The P&L conversation on a Tuesday.

Compliance & tasks

Ten categories with photo evidence, numeric readings with min/max bands, expiring-certificate reminders, shift-attached tasks. The EHO visit stops being an event.

Team & HR

Right-to-work, NMW age bands, hourly rate, NI, tax code, bank details, per-area permissions, multi-site with per-venue rates. Column-level privacy in the database, not the UI.

Communication

Noticeboard with read receipts, direct messages, emergency-pin banner, push notifications with server-side opt-outs. Not the WhatsApp group.

Events & diary

Private parties, functions, sports fixtures — inline on the rota with covers, brief, task list and thread. Venue setup, device pairing, white-label branding.

The one-paragraph version

Why they're together, not separate subscriptions.

The rota knows what labour costs, because it holds everyone's hourly rate. The till knows what you took, so labour % is a live number, not a month-end guess. The clock knows who actually worked, so payroll is built from punches rather than memory. The stock knows what the till sold, so it depletes itself and GP is real. Compliance hangs off the same shift checklist staff already tick. No competitor at this price point closes that loop for a single-site independent — and it costs £49 to £149 a venue, on hardware the venue already owns.
