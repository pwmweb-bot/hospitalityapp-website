WHAT IT DOES

Points, stamps,
and a scan.

  • Off until you switch it on. A per-venue setting. Off by default.
  • A card in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. The guest carries a QR. Staff scan it on the till, or on a staff phone.
  • Points per pound. They earn at a rate the venue sets, on the bill. Tips never earn points.
  • A points discount. Redeem points through the discount button already on the till.
  • A product stamp. Buy 5 Guinness, get 1 free, for example. Progress shows on the card, such as Guinness 3/5. Redeeming adds the free item at £0, with VAT handled.
  • Scan from the staff app. Use the Scan button, or scan from an open bill. After payment, staff can still scan to attach the card, when loyalty is on and no card is on the bill yet.
  • Managers can adjust balances. A manager can change the points on a card.
  • One site, or a group. The venue can scope points to one site, or a group of sites.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Which plan includes loyalty?

Loyalty is included on every plan. Book a 15-minute demo and we will show how a card sits on a bill.

Do tips earn points?

No. Points earn per £ of the bill, at a rate the venue sets. Tips never earn points.

How does a free drink land on the bill?

A product stamp, such as buy 5 Guinness, get 1 free, shows progress on the card. Redeeming adds the free item at £0, with VAT handled. A points reward goes through the discount button already on the till.

Can staff attach a card after payment?

Yes, when loyalty is on and no card is on the bill yet. They can also scan from the staff app Scan button, or from an open bill.
