What it does

The weekly tronc, without transcription.

  • Card tips pull automatically from the till — no transcription. Every tip taken on the terminal is already in the pool total.
  • Cash tips entered manually — one number, one screen.
  • Calculate → Review → Finalise, producing a tronc statement with per-person allocations and a Draft / Finalised badge.
  • Managers only — tronc is not a staff-facing calculation screen.
  • Dated, itemised, downloadable — the record you'd hand to a worker who asked or a tribunal that ordered.
Why it matters

Tronc is a legal minefield.

The Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2023 requires a written policy, fair and transparent allocation, and a durable record of every tip and every allocation. Any worker can request their allocation records; any tribunal can enforce. The regulator's test isn't your intent — it's the paper trail.

A dated, itemised, finalised statement per week is the paper trail. Everything else is a hope. The Finalise button is the difference between "we split fairly" and proof that you did.
