The rooms,
without the fuss.

A plain way to run a few rooms above the bar. Same system as the till.

DIARY

Who is in which room.

A room-by-date diary. Block a date when a room is closed. A stay cannot sit on top of another stay.

CHECK-IN

In, and out again.

Check guests in and out, with the UK guest register (the fields in the Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972).

HOUSEKEEPING

Ready, or not.

Each room is clean, dirty, inspected or out of order.

RATES

Room only, or B&B.

A rate for the room, and a minimum stay when the weekend needs one.

Straight onto
the room bill.

One bill to settle at check-out. No paper chits. Nothing missed.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

How does a round get onto the room bill?

Take it at the bar, or at the table on a staff phone. From that open tab, choose the room. It lands on the folio, and the guest pays it with the room at check-out. Staff phone ordering is the same idea as the till, carried to the table.

Is this a second system?

No. Rooms sit with the till, the rota, payroll and reports. One login. One bill for the stay.

Can two stays share a room on the same night?

No. The diary blocks that. You can also block a date yourself, for maintenance or a closed room.

Does it connect to booking websites?

No. Stays are entered in the diary, or requested on your own room page if you switch that on.

Can a guest pay for the room online?

No. Check-out is settled in the venue by card, cash or bank transfer, and the invoice is emailed.

What does room management cost?

It is not on the published plan prices. See the plans, then book a 15-min demo and talk to us.

Who is it for?

Pubs with rooms, inns and small hotels. Pubs and bars, hotels and B&Bs can all start from the same app.
