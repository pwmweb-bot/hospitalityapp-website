WHAT YOU CAN RUN

The stay, from
the first night.

Built for a handful of rooms above the bar, not a separate system on another login.

01 / RATES

Room only, or bed and breakfast.

Set a room type, a room-only rate and a B&B rate, and a minimum stay when the weekend needs one.

02 / DIARY

One room, one night, one stay.

A room-by-date diary, with dates you can block. A stay cannot be booked on top of another stay.

03 / CHECK-IN

The guest register, at the desk.

Check guests in and out with the fields the Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972 asks a hotel to keep.

04 / FOLIO

One bill for the whole stay.

Room nights sit on the folio at 20% VAT. At checkout, settle by card, cash or bank transfer and email an itemised VAT invoice.

05 / HOUSEKEEPING

Which room is actually ready.

Each room is clean, dirty, inspected or out of order, so the desk and housekeeping share one status.

06 / REPORTS

Occupancy, next to takings.

Occupancy, average daily rate (ADR) and revenue per available room (RevPAR), alongside what the bar and the kitchen took.

THE UK GUEST REGISTER

Check-in that
keeps the record.

The register follows the Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972. It is filled in at check-in and check-out, and it stays with the stay.

  • Every guest aged 16 or over. Full name, nationality and the date of arrival.
  • Where the Order asks for a passport record. The document number and where it was issued.
  • On departure. The date they leave and, where the Order requires it, their next destination.

This is the record the desk keeps in the app. It is not a substitute for reading the Order, or for advice on a particular guest.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Is this a second login?

No. Till, rooms, rota, payroll and reports use the same login. The bar tab and the room bill are the same system.

Can two stays share a room on the same night?

No. The diary blocks that. You can also block a date yourself, for maintenance or a closed room.

Can a guest pay for the room online?

No. Checkout is settled in the venue by card, cash or bank transfer, and the guest is emailed an itemised VAT invoice.

Does it connect to booking websites?

No. Stays are entered in the diary, or requested on your own room page if you switch that on.

What does room management cost?

It is not on the published plan prices. See the plans, then book a 15-min demo and talk to us.

Who is it for?

Pubs with rooms, inns and small hotels. Pubs and bars, hotels and B&Bs can all start from the same app.
