Bar and food

Straight onto
the room bill.

One bill for the stay. No paper chits. Nothing missed.

  1. 01

    Take the order

    At the bar on the till, or at the table on a staff phone. Food and drink, on the same tab.

  2. 02

    Choose the room

    From that open tab, charge it to the guest’s room.

  3. 03

    It lands on the folio

    The order sits on that stay, next to the room nights.

  4. 04

    Settle what is left

    Anything not already paid — the room, if nothing was taken at booking, plus bar and food — is settled at check-out by card, cash or bank transfer.

  5. 05

    The invoice is emailed

    An itemised VAT invoice. Room nights are at 20% VAT. Bar and food keep the VAT already on the till.

When they book

No deposit, a deposit, or pay in full.

On the deposit step of a new stay, choose one of three. A card payment goes to your own Stripe account.

  • No deposit. The guest pays at check-out.
  • Pay deposit now. The guest pays a deposit by card when they book.
  • Pay in full now. The guest pays the whole stay by card when they book.

Hospitality App takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. Booking a stay walks through the step.

The guest’s pay step on the room booking page, once a deposit is due. Example data for The Cock Inn. Nothing is charged.

Good to know

A few practical answers.

How does bar and food get onto the room bill?

From an open tab on the till, or at the table on a staff phone, choose the room. It sits on the stay and is paid at check-out. Staff phone ordering is the same idea as the till, carried to the table.

Is this a second system?

No. Rooms sit with the till, the rota, payroll and reports. One login.

Can two stays share a room on the same night?

No. The diary stops that, and out-of-order dates block the room too.

Can a guest pay for the room online?

Yes. When the stay is booked, choose no deposit, a card deposit, or pay in full. The card payment goes to your own Stripe account. Hospitality App takes no commission. Stripe’s own fees still apply. Anything still owed, including bar and food on the room, is settled at check-out by card, cash or bank transfer, and the invoice is emailed.

Does it connect to booking websites?

No. Stays are entered in Hospitality App. There is no connection to Booking.com, Expedia or Airbnb.

Where do I learn how to use it?

Each screen has a ? button with the guide for that screen. The full set is in the rooms help, starting with the diary, housekeeping and capacity.

What does room management cost?

It is included on every plan. Charging bar and food to a room needs the till (Complete and up). A function room booked by the session is Spaces, included on every plan.

Who is it for?

Pubs with rooms, inns and small hotels. Pubs and bars, hotels and B&Bs can all start from the same app.
