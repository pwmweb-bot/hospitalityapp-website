What it does

What goes into the payroll run.

  • Payroll report for any date range — hand a bookkeeper a finished pack instead of a shoebox.
  • Payment basis — planned hours / exact minutes / rounded to 15. Your call, applied consistently.
  • Three views — itemised by person, weekly summary, summary table. Checking one person, signing off a week and exporting are different jobs.
  • Fix clock data inline — missing clock-out? Add it in the report. No context switch to another screen.
  • Email the payroll pack — HTML summary + itemised CSV, sent to a verified payroll address. You can't leak wages to a typo.
  • CSV export — summary or itemised, for whatever your accountant actually uses.
  • All-branches payroll — one run across every site an owner holds; multi-site staff consolidated with their per-venue rates.
  • "My pay" for staff — day / week / month, scheduled vs actual per shift, hours × their rate, in the app. Cuts the most common recurring question a manager gets.

Off-rota worked time is accounted for separately and visibly rather than folded into a total — so a staff member can see it, and a manager has to decide about it.

Why it matters

The pay conversation, when there isn't one.

The pay-day argument is almost never about the money itself — it's about the evidence. "Was I here at nine or ten?" "Did the swap go through?" "Why is Tuesday four hours and not five?" When the answer is a screen the staff member has been looking at themselves all fortnight, the argument doesn't happen. When it's a scribble on a printout, the manager loses.

The verified-recipient email pack is a small guardrail with an outsized effect: leaked wage runs are a real category of hospitality data breach, and they almost always trace back to a typo'd email address being trusted. Refusing to send until the recipient has clicked once is the difference.

How it joins to the rest

Payroll sits on three modules underneath.

Time & attendance

Immutable punches; discrepancies queue resolves messy sessions before they hit payroll.

Rota & scheduling

Hourly rates on the rota feed the pay basis directly. Planned-vs-actual variance is a two-click check.

Tips & tronc

A dated, itemised, finalised tronc statement sits alongside the payroll pack. Two records, one truth.
